Japanese traditional House

Genkan (玄関)

traditional Japanese entryway or foyer area of a house, apartment, or building. It serves as a space where people remove their shoes before stepping into the main living area..

Washitsu (和室

Tatami (畳) Flooring,Shoji (障子) or Fusuma (襖),Tokonoma (床の間).

ふとん (Futon)

Futon (ふとん) is the traditional Japanese bed. It is a mattress which is laid directly on the floor (usually tatami). The Futon is made with cotton or synthetic fibers and is folded when not in use.
