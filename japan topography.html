japan topography and natural features

topography

  • Japan's topography is highly mountainous, with about 73% of the land covered by mountains
  • Mount Fuji is Japan’s highest peak at 3,776 meters.
  • four main islands (Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, and Shikoku
  • apan has many rivers, including the Shinano River

seasons

  • Haru (春) is the Japanese word for spring.
  • Natsu (夏) is the Japanese word for summer, typically spanning from June to August
  • Aki (秋) is the Japanese word for autumn (fall), typically from September to November. It's a season known for its cooler weather, beautiful foliage, and harvest time
  • Fuyu (冬) is the Japanese word for winter, typically from December to February. It's a season characterized by cold temperatures, snow in some regions, and cozy indoor activities.

Mount Fuji (富士山, Fujisan)

  • Japan’s highest and most iconic mountain, standing at 3,776 meters
  • located on the Honshu island, near Tokyo and Yokohama.
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site, celebrated for its natural beauty and cultural importance
