japanese clothes

kimona

long T-shaped robe with wide sleeves, wrapped around the body and secured with an obi (belt).

Furisode (振袖)

Furisode (振袖) is a type of formal kimono worn by unmarried women in Japan. It is known for its long, flowing sleeves, which can be over 100 cm in length.

tomesode (留袖)

A tomesode (留袖) is a formal type of kimono typically worn by married women

yukata (浴衣)

casual, lightweight kimono typically worn during the summer months in Japan, especially for festivals, fireworks displays, and hot spring trips.

komon (小紋)

type of kimono that features a patterned design covering the entire garment

shiromuku (白無垢)

A shiromuku (白無垢) is a traditional white kimono worn by brides during a Japanese wedding..
