Decoding Japanese Food

Understanding Japanese Food Terms

DON

Refers to a rice bowl dish, derived from "donburi." Can be served with pork, beef, chicken, or egg. (e.g., Gyudon, Katsudon)

KARAAGE

A deep-frying technique, usually for chicken, where pieces are marinated in soy sauce, garlic, and ginger before frying.

YAKI

Refers to grilling or frying, as in Yakisoba, Takoyaki, and Okonomiyaki.

SASHIMI

Raw fish or meat sliced into thin pieces, often paired with soy sauce and wasabi.

KATSU

Breaded and deep-fried cutlet (pork, chicken, etc.), often eaten before exams because "katsu" sounds like "to win."
